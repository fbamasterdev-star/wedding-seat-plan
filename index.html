🎉 Welcome to EverAfter SeatStudio!

Thank you for purchasing the commercial-grade wedding seating suite. This digital product is 100% offline-capable, serverless, and private. Follow the quick guide below to configure your wedding, generate scannable QR signs, and export high-res printables.

1 Host the Mobile Viewer on GitHub Pages (Takes 60 Seconds - 100% Free)

To let your guests scan the QR code on their smartphones with zero database fees:

  1. Create a free account at github.com.
  2. Create a new public repository (e.g., wedding-viewer).
  3. Upload the single file from the viewer/index.html folder.
  4. Go to Repository Settings → Pages and set Branch to main, then click Save.
  5. Your free viewer URL is now: https://yourusername.github.io/wedding-viewer/.
  6. Paste this URL into Tab 3 (QR Sign & Mobile Portal) → "GitHub Pages Viewer Base URL". Your QR code updates automatically!

2 Standalone Offline Kiosk Mode (Bonus Mode C)

Don't want to use GitHub Pages? In Tab 3, click "Download Standalone Mobile Portal HTML". This creates a self-contained guest-lookup.html file with your complete wedding data baked inside. You can open it on an iPad, laptop, or venue touch kiosk with ZERO internet connection!

4 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What if guests scan the QR code and the venue has weak cell reception?
A: Tab 5 includes an Emergency Day-Of Usher Checklist ready to print on A4 paper with checkboxes, party sizes, and allergy notes. Print 2 copies for your coordinators as a bulletproof zero-tech fallback!
Q: Can guests see other guests' private information?
A: No. Guests only see their own table assignment, their direct party members, their meal, and the glowing venue map. No phone numbers, emails, or personal data are stored or shared.
Q: How do I print the posters at a print shop (Staples, Walgreens, Printful)?
A: In Tab 4 (Printable Posters), select your paper size (e.g. 24x36"), enable crop marks, and click Print / Save as PDF. Choose "Save as PDF" at 100% scale and send the PDF directly to your printer!
